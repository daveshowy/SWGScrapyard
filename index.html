Each vehicle has innate capacity for the four systems (the B values on the datapad with no components installed), so the same parts can balance on one vehicle and not another.

A (drain) = the balancing raws summed across your components; B (capacity) = the vehicle's innate capacity plus the raw on the one component that supplies it (chassis integrity, cooling, stabilization, power generation). Balanced when A ≤ B. Datapad format with no parts installed confirms A starts at 0.
